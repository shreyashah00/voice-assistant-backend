ACME CORP - EMPLOYEE & SALES POLICY HANDBOOK

1. WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE
- Standard office working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM NPT.
- Employees are eligible for flexible remote work up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
- Overtime requires prior written approval from the department lead.

2. LEAVE & TIME OFF POLICY
- Annual Paid Leave: 18 days per calendar year.
- Sick Leave: 12 days per calendar year.
- Maternity Leave: 14 weeks paid leave.
- Paternity Leave: 2 weeks paid leave.
- Unplanned leaves must be notified to HR before 10:00 AM on the day of absence.

3. SALES & CLIENT COMMISSION POLICY
- Base Sales Target: Every sales executive has a monthly target of $10,000 in closed-won deals.
- Commission Tier 1: 5% commission on all sales up to 100% of target.
- Commission Tier 2: 8% commission on any revenue achieved beyond 100% of target.
- Sales Payout Date: Commissions are calculated on the 1st of each month and paid alongside regular salary.

4. REFUND & RETURN POLICY FOR CLIENTS
- Software subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of purchase if usage is under 10%.
- Hardware products have a 30-day return window provided items are unopened and in original packaging.
- Refund requests must be submitted via email to support@acmecorp.com.

5. CODE OF CONDUCT & CONFIDENTIALITY
- All company data, client lead lists, and software codebases are strictly confidential.
- Employees must not disclose proprietary information or engage with direct competitors during employment.
